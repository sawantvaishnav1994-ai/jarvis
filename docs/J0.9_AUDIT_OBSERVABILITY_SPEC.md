# J0.9 Audit, Observability, Traceability & Forensic Evidence

Protected baseline: J0.1-J0.8 at `351fbbbcf9eb4cfd4d524ad6e5fb5ffad3b0f4de`.

## Permanent rules

Audit evidence records authority and outcome; it never creates authority. J0.8 events remain operational signals while J0.9 audit records are authoritative evidence. Models, agents, tools and external providers cannot mutate authoritative audit history. PostgreSQL is the durable audit source of truth; telemetry exporters are optional processors.

J0.9 separates four concerns: append-oriented authoritative audit, distributed trace/correlation, bounded metrics/health, and safe diagnostics/Owner forensic inspection.

## Evidence

V3 audit records are namespaced/versioned, Owner/project scoped, D0-D4 only, minimum-necessary, append-only through application permissions and database triggers, and chained with SHA-256 over deterministic canonical data plus the previous hash. D5 and secret-bearing content are rejected before persistence. Privacy actions use append-only redaction/tombstone evidence rather than rewriting history.

Integrity verification checks schema, sequence continuity, previous-hash continuity and record hashes. Checkpoints capture a bounded stream range and chain head. Startup/control-plane verification fails closed on integrity errors and reports DEGRADED rather than silently repairing evidence.

## Observability

Trace context reuses correlation/causation semantics. Completed spans are provider-neutral and exportable. Structured logs are sanitized before output. Metrics use bounded label keys and values, capped series, counters and latency observations. Liveness and readiness are distinct. Exporters are bounded and non-authoritative: exporter failure degrades observability but cannot erase authoritative audit.

## Access and reliability

Audit reads/verification/export are Owner scoped and capability gated. Privileged inspection/export itself can produce bounded meta-audit evidence. Security-critical audited database mutations use the same PostgreSQL transaction as their audit append. Runtime roles receive SELECT/INSERT only on J0.9 audit tables and cannot update/delete them.

No J0.10 recovery work, Foundation v1 GO or J1 work is part of J0.9.
